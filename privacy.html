Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

What we collect

We collect your name and phone number when you are added to a NeverAlone Circle by a device owner. We also collect button press events including the time of the press and the device identifier.

How we use it

We use your information solely to send you alert notifications when a NeverAlone button is pressed. We do not use your information for marketing purposes.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share your mobile phone number with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not share your data with third parties except as required to deliver the service — specifically Twilio for SMS and phone call delivery, and our secure cloud database for storing alert records.

SMS messaging

You are added to a Circle by a device owner and consent to receive alerts by confirming your opt-in. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for support. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on button presses.

Data retention

Your information is retained for as long as you are an active Circle member. You may request removal at any time by replying STOP to any alert message.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at trainedwellness@gmail.com.
