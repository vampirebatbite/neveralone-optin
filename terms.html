Last updated: July 10, 2026

Service description

NeverAlone is a personal emergency notification service. When a NeverAlone button is pressed, the service sends an SMS alert and places a phone call to designated Circle members — personal contacts chosen by the device owner.

Program name

NeverAlone Alert Notifications

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. You will receive a text message and phone call each time the NeverAlone button associated with your Circle is pressed. There is no fixed frequency — messages are triggered by button presses only.

Message and data rates

Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Opt-out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving alerts. You will receive a confirmation that you have been removed. After opting out, you will receive no further messages unless re-added to a Circle by a device owner.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us at losercopilot@gmail.com.

Notification only

NeverAlone confirms that a notification was sent. It does not guarantee that any Circle member will respond, and it does not connect to emergency services. NeverAlone is not a substitute for calling 911 in an emergency.

Limitation of liability

NeverAlone is a communication tool only. We are not liable for any harm resulting from a failure to receive, read, or respond to an alert.
